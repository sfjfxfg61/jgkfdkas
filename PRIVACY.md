# Privacy notes

The bot stores Telegram user id, username/name, language/region, public/private channel state, subscription/payment metadata, messages sent to the bot, sales events, follow-up jobs, traffic attribution and admin notes in Supabase.

Chat replies are manual. User messages are delivered to the service administrator.

Traffic uploaders only receive aggregate statistics for users attributed to their personal link; they do not receive customer identities or message contents.

Telegram Stars handles payment processing; the bot does not receive card details.
