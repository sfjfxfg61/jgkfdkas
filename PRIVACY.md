## Data collected

The service processes Telegram user ID, username and display name, language, referral code, selected pricing region, settings, conversation messages, generated long-term memories, usage counters, subscription status, payment identifiers, and product analytics events. Telegram and payment providers may process additional data under their own policies.

## Why data is used

Data is used to provide conversations and memory, operate subscriptions, prevent abuse, support users, measure product performance, and comply with legal obligations. Payment card details are not stored by this bot.
